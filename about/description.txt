DPS Meter

A client-side combat meter for Shape of Dreams.

Features:
- Current DPS and total damage, with skill and Essence breakdowns
- HPS, healing totals, and healing-source breakdowns
- BPS, barrier totals, and barrier-source breakdowns
- Party DPS/total comparisons and contribution percentages
- Recent run history with character and run details
- Optional display filters for overkill, overheal, and Essence of Mortality damage
- Movable overlay, settings, and context-menu actions

The meter observes the game's client-side combat events and attributes activity using the game's actor and skill relationships. It does not modify the game's damage calculations.
