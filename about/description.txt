DPS Meter

A client-side damage meter for Shape of Dreams.

Current features:
- Total damage
- DPS
- Damage breakdown by skill
- Damage breakdown by Essence
- Percentage contribution for skills and Essences
- Reset button
- Hide button
- Console commands: dps_reset and dps_meter

The meter listens to the game's existing client damage event and attributes damage through the game's actor tree rather than changing damage calculations.
